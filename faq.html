Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

Is PDF Tools free?

Yes, PDF Tools is completely free to use.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Your images are processed directly in your browser. We do not store your files.

Which image formats are supported?

You can convert common image formats like JPG and PNG into PDF files.

Can I change the order of images?

Yes. Use the arrows to reorder your images before creating the PDF.

Can I use PDF Tools on my phone?

Yes. The website works on mobile phones, tablets and computers.
